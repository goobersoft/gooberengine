# Seedling sounds. A step is: note, wave (0-f, n = noise), volume 0-7, effect
# (. none, s slide, v vibrato, d drop, < fade in, > fade out, a arpeggio).

# 0 picking up a seed: a quick rising chime
sfx 0 speed 3 length 4 loop 0 0
  C-5 3 6 .  E-5 3 6 .  G-5 3 5 .  C-6 3 4 >

# 1 a beetle bump: a noisy thud that drops away
sfx 1 speed 4 length 5 loop 0 0
  E-3 n 7 d  C-3 n 6 d  A-2 0 5 d  F-2 0 3 >  C-2 0 2 >

# 2 the garden is full: a little fanfare
sfx 2 speed 6 length 12 loop 0 0
  C-4 5 6 .  E-4 5 6 .  G-4 5 6 .  C-5 5 6 .  --- 0 0 .  G-4 5 5 .  C-5 6 7 a  C-5 6 7 a
  C-5 6 6 v  C-5 6 5 v  C-5 6 4 >  C-5 6 2 >
