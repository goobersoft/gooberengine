0. Definitions.

"Engine" means GooberEngine and GooberBASIC, in source or object form, as
published by their copyright holders.

"Cart" means a program written to be run by the Engine, together with its
data: its GooberBASIC source files (.gb), tiles, maps, fonts, sound effects,
music, save data and any other files that belong to it, whether in a folder,
in an archive (.zip, .tar, .tar.gz) or in any other form.

"Player Runtime" means the runtimes the Engine provides for running a Cart on
its own (such as the files in runtimes/: the Linux and Windows player
executables and the web player's player.js, player.wasm and index.html),
including the parts of GooberBASIC and of any libraries compiled into them.

"Exported Game" means a Cart combined with a Player Runtime, whether by the
Engine's EXPORT command or by any other means that gives the same result.

"Example Code" means the Carts, examples and templates distributed with the
Engine (such as those in carts/ and in GooberBASIC's examples/).


1. Carts are not covered by the GPLv3.

A Cart is not a work based on the Engine merely because it is written in
GooberBASIC, is run by the Engine, calls the Engine's commands and constants,
or is made or edited with the Engine's editors. You may license and
distribute your Carts under terms of your choice.


2. Exported Games.

You may distribute an Exported Game under terms of your choice, provided the
Player Runtime in it is unmodified. You need not meet the GPLv3's conditions
for that Player Runtime when you distribute it as part of an Exported Game:
in particular, you need not provide its source code or a written offer of it.

If you modify a Player Runtime, the modified Player Runtime is covered by the
GPLv3 as usual, but the Cart in your Exported Game still falls under
section 1.


3. Example Code.

You may copy Example Code, in whole or in part, into your own Carts, and
distribute it as part of them under terms of your choice.


4. What this exception does not do.

This exception does not change the license of the Engine itself. Anyone who
distributes the Engine, or a modified version of it, other than as a Player
Runtime inside an Exported Game, must do so under the GPLv3.

Third-party libraries in the Player Runtime keep their own licenses. SDL2,
which is linked into the Linux and Windows players and, through Emscripten,
into the web player, is under the zlib license, which places no conditions on
distributing it in binary form.


5. Modified versions of the Engine.

If you modify the Engine, you may extend this exception to your version, but
you are not obliged to. If you do not wish to, delete this exception
from your version.
